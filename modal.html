Flowline · Task 1

Feature

Smart Prioritization

Flowline looks at due dates, dependencies, and how long a task has been sitting untouched, then quietly nudges the important stuff to the top of your list. You can always override it by hand — the drag list on the Reorder page is exactly that override.

Feature

Real-time Validation

Every field checks itself as you type — no waiting until you hit submit to find out your phone number is missing a digit. Green means go, red tells you exactly what to fix, and the submit button stays locked until everything checks out.

Feature

Seamless Reordering

Pick up any task and drop it where it belongs. As you drag, a line shows exactly where it'll land, and the moment you let go, every priority number updates to match the new order.
